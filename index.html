About

I'm a marketing and communications specialist who works at the seam between brand and business — where a campaign idea has to survive contact with a KPI.

Across 4+ years in fintech, banking and tech, I've marketed digital products to audiences as different as teenagers opening their first account and women running businesses out of Instagram DMs. I run the full arc: strategy, the content that carries it, and the reporting that proves whether it worked.

My work lives on the ALAT ecosystem — campaign strategy, performance reporting, internal comms and content production — but the method travels: understand the audience, sharpen the positioning, ship, measure, adjust.
