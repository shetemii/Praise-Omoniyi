About

I don't just plan campaigns, I run them: from the idea on a whiteboard to the post that goes live, the email that gets opened, the event that goes off without a hitch.

I'm a content and digital marketing strategist with 5+ years of experience, an MBA in Marketing from Miva Open University, and ANIPR accreditation from the Nigerian Institute of Public Relations. My work sits at the point where a marketing idea has to become something real. I handle content strategy, social media, email and SMS marketing, and event management, then bring it all together through hands-on campaign execution, audience segmentation, and honest performance reporting on what actually moved the needle.

Across fintech, banking and tech, I've marketed digital products to audiences as different as teenagers opening their first account and women running businesses out of Instagram DMs, always working the same loop: plan, execute, report, improve.

Alongside my client-side work, I bring agency experience from Teksight Media Agency, where I worked on brand and influencer campaigns for TECNO, Infinix and vivo, and contributed to the ideation behind the Lagos SDG initiative, giving me a feel for both sides of the brief: the client's KPI and the agency's creative.

Anniversary Campaign · Content & Events Campaign originator · Content · Interviews · Event execution

Wema at 80: "80 Days to 80"

Challenge

An 80th anniversary is easy to reduce to a plaque and a party. The brief was to make eight decades of institutional history feel alive, credible, and worth a reader's time, then land it in a flagship event befitting the milestone.

Approach

Originated the "80 Days to 80" concept: 80 days of articles and blog content tracing the bank's growth, built on original interviews with past MDs, EDs, the chairman, staff and partners, and wrote a portion of that content myself. Carried the campaign through to the grand anniversary dinner, drafting the partner invitations, managing partner engagement, executing the event on the day, and writing scripts for the videographers covering it.
